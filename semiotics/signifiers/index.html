semiotics/signifiers/

Terms the corpus coins and treats as load-bearing. Each is presented here as a signifier — noting what work it does in the text — not as an established legal or analytical category. FSD ontology classes are noted where a term has been formalised as an OWL class.

Nullification Loop · Lúba Neamhnithe

OBSERVATION Corpus definition: "a closed five-stage system (acknowledgement → deferral → remit displacement → sufficiency assertion → record suppression) producing non-determination regardless of personnel."
source: meinhardt-initiative-compact-sep10.md
fsd:NullificationLoop (FSD-Ontology-2026 §4.6)
Ontology note: "Not a class of act but a pattern of acts — identified by aggregate conduct, not any single instance." The ontology treats this as a structural attractor, explicitly not requiring proof of intent or coordination between actors.
source: FSD-Ontology-2026.md §4.6
INTERPRETATION The signifier does two things simultaneously: it names a pattern of acts (the five stages) and it asserts the pattern's causal invariance ("regardless of personnel"). The second move is the stronger claim — it pre-empts the obvious counter-argument that different personnel would produce different outputs. Whether that invariance holds is an empirical question the corpus treats as established and this repository treats as not yet independently verified.
See also: posiwid/system-observations/ · behaviour/non-actions/

Silence Veto · Veto Tosta

OBSERVATION Corpus definition: "procedural silence functioning as substantive adverse determination without notice, reasons, or hearing."
source: meinhardt-initiative-compact-sep10.md
fsd:SilenceVeto (FSD-Ontology-2026 §4.5)
Ontology test: established by practical effect, not motivation. Engages Art. 41 CFR (right to be heard; obligation to give reasons).
source: FSD-Ontology-2026.md §4.5
INTERPRETATION The term converts a passive condition (no response) into an active one (veto). That conversion is a rhetorical move that matters: it assigns agency to the silence, making it reviewable as an act rather than dismissable as mere delay. Whether Irish or EU administrative law actually treats prolonged silence as a determination-equivalent is the question the corpus's legal arguments are built to answer affirmatively.
See also: semiotics/absences/

Ontic Collapse · Clis Ontach

OBSERVATION Corpus definition: the most acute form of structural displacement — the institutional capacity required to discharge a statutory obligation has ceased to exist within the public body architecture.
source: gatekeeping-brief-v4-8b-interim.md; feasible-region.html §09
fsd:OnticCollapse (FSD-Ontology-2026 §4.5)
Subclass of fsd:StructuralDisplacement. SWRL Rule 6: established when (a) no single body holds the full competence required and (b) no statutory mechanism assembles the partial competences held by separate bodies. Applied to the existing record: CA function distributed across DETE (licensing), DFA (regime memberships), DoD (security assessment) with no statutory coordination mechanism.
source: FSD-Ontology-2026.md §4.5, §12
INTERPRETATION "Ontic" is doing philosophical weight-lifting here: it shifts the failure from the empirical ("no one acted") to the metaphysical ("no agent exists who could act"). This has a precise rhetorical function — it moves the remedy from holding existing actors accountable to requiring the creation of a new institutional actor. The Hart incomplete-contracts reading in feasible-region.html §09 makes the same move in the language of economics: "the architecture is silent on who holds the residual right." Both framings converge on the same structural prescription.
See also: posiwid/purpose-inferred-from-behaviour/ · semiotics/narratives/

Foreclosure by Proxy · Dúnadh trí Sheachvótáil

fsd:ForeClosureByProxy (FSD-Ontology-2026 §4.4)
Named instance: DOD-MO-01201-2025 (4 Feb 2026). Body issuing the determination (DoD) held no CA designation; conclusions relied upon (OEP) also held no CA designation; formally rebutted conclusions were not engaged.
source: FSD-Ontology-2026.md §4.4
INTERPRETATION "Proxy" carries a specific legal resonance — it implies substitution within a delegated authority structure. The corpus uses it to argue that DoD's determination was not merely wrong but ultra vires: void because issued by an actor with no standing in the relevant statutory framework. Whether that argument holds under Order 84 RSC is the High Court question; this page records only the signification move.
See also: behaviour/closures/ · corpus/decisions/

Presidency Paradox

OBSERVATION Corpus definition: "Ireland chairs Council proceedings on dual-use controls from 1 Jul 2026 while holding a 20-year CA vacancy."
source: meinhardt-initiative-compact-sep10.md
INTERPRETATION "Paradox" frames a coincidence of timing as a logical contradiction. Whether it constitutes a legal conflict (Ireland presiding over compliance discussions while itself non-compliant) or merely an irony (a matter of politics and optics) is a question the corpus treats as the former and this repository leaves open. The institutional fact — Ireland holds the Presidency during the Art. 26 evaluation window — is independently verifiable.
See also: behaviour/delays/ · posiwid/system-observations/
