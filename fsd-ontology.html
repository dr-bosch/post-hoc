4.1 CORE SUBMISSIONs.2
fsd:SubmissionAighneacht
Formal written communication to a public body identifying the submitter, setting out material of potential national utility, inviting determination. ⊑ lkif:LegalDocument, min-cardinality 1 on hasSubmitter.
SubmissionOfNationalUtility
4-part test s.2(1): (a) national significance (b) invokes the duty (c) identified person (d) plausible body connection. Saving: retrospective cases waive (b) if (a)(c)(d) hold.
DualUseSubmission
Asserts dual-use character under Reg. (EU) 2021/821 Art.2(1) → triggers Art.9(2) competent-authority assessment duty.
HighRiskAISubmission
Asserts high-risk AI system under Reg. (EU) 2024/1689 → engages conformity duties, Arts. 9, 13, 14, 27.
TestableSubmission
Has an operational / executable / testable component. s.5A: no sufficiency assertion available without a prior arranged testing opportunity.
4.2 INSTITUTIONALs.1
fsd:PublicBodyComhlacht Poiblí
Any body bearing a determination obligation: ministers, departments, statutory bodies, Oireachtas clerks (Art.15 savings), designated competent authorities. ⊑ cpv:PublicOrganisation, lkif:PublicBody.
CompetentAuthority
Reg. (EU) 2021/821 Art.9(1) designee; must maintain assessment capacity (Art.9(2)). IE: DFA 2009–2024‑08‑22 → DETE thereafter.
Ombudsman
Residual determination authority, Part VI. On loop condition: investigate, report, apply to the High Court.
fsd:IndependentAuditor
s.5C appointee for technical audits. ⊑ foaf:Agent. Independence conditions s.5B — no financial, contractual, or institutional interest.
4.3 DETERMINATIONs.3
fsd:DeterminationCinneadh
Written act: acceptance / rejection-with-reasons / referral-with-legal-basis. Silence, acknowledgement, sufficiency assertion excluded by s.3(3). owl:disjointWith NonDetermination.
AcceptanceDetermination
Accepts submission, opens statutory evaluation. s.2(1)(a).
RejectionDetermination
Rejects with specific, reviewable reasons that engage the submitted corpus — generic domain familiarity does not qualify. s.2(1)(b).
ReferralDetermination
Refers to a named competent body with stated legal basis; receiving body's 12-month clock starts on transmission. s.2(1)(c), s.4.
fsd:NonDeterminationNeamh-Chinneadh
No determination within the s.3(1) period — established regardless of conduct form (silence / ack / sufficiency / amnesia).
4.4 PROHIBITED PRACTICESPts II–III
fsd:ProhibitedPracticeCleachtas Toirmiscthe
Superclass of five prohibited acts. Each is categorically wrong, not a lesser determination — none can satisfy the s.3 duty.
RemitDisplacement
"Not our remit" — without (a) naming the body, (b) 30-day transmission, or (c) submitter notice. s.4.
SufficiencyAssertion
"Existing provision is sufficient" — issued without evaluating the specific submitted corpus. s.5.
AdministrativeAmnesia
Successive officers meet the submission as if new — passive reset, no accumulated obligation. Distinct from forgetting (active). s.6.
DesignedForgetting
Active deletion of lodged correspondence without determination/transmission/record. Engages CFR Art.41 access-to-file. Instance: JCEUA deletion, Oct 2025. s.7.
ForeclosureByProxy
Determination issued by a body lacking CA designation, relying on another non-CA body's conclusions. Instance: DOD-MO-01201-2025, 4 Feb 2026. s.8A.
4.5 STRUCTURAL CONDITIONSs.2A
fsd:StructuralConditionCoinníoll Struchtúrach
Systemic capacity failure. Four types defined by s.2A — mutually reinforcing, not mutually exclusive.
NormativeCollapse
Procedurally valid outputs issue while substantive obligations stay permanently unmet — a self-sustaining form/substance gap. s.2A(2)(a).
SilenceVeto
Silence functions as a substantive adverse determination — no notice, hearing, or reasons. No intent required, effect-test only. Engages CFR Art.41 / Re Haughey [1971]. s.2A(2)(b).
StructuralDisplacement
Required competence is split across bodies with no assembly mechanism. Established from the statutory architecture alone. s.2A(2)(c).
↳ OnticCollapse
Acute StructuralDisplacement: no body holds full competence and no mechanism assembles the parts. Identifies the level of intervention (legislative/executive) required. s.2A(1A).
4.6 LOOP ARCHITECTUREPart VI
fsd:NullificationLoopLúba Neamhnithe
5-stage attractor, personnel-independent: Acknowledgement → Deferral → Remit Displacement → Sufficiency Assertion → Record Suppression. Output invariant: no determination.
fsd:LoopCondition
Triggered under s.10B when ≥3 bodies decline on the merits, or ≥24 months pass with no body holding carriage. Vests the residual obligation in the Ombudsman.
fsd:ResidualObligation
Ombudsman must: (a) investigate the merits (b) report under s.10C(3)(b) (c) apply to the High Court if undetermined 6 months post-report.
fsd:LoopClosure
Ends via: body determination pre-application / High Court order s.10C(7) / ontic-collapse finding. Entered on the s.10E public register.
fsd:TechnicalAudit
s.5C: assesses operational capacity, executability, replicability, EU 2021/821 Art.9(2) & AI Act Arts.13–14 compliance. Does not rule on the merits.

Interoperability, vocabulary, integration

EIF Interoperability Layers

LEGALThe duty of determination becomes justiciable across member-state law (CFR Art.41/47, TEU Art.2/4(3)); mapped to machine-readable axioms.
ORGANISATIONALLoopCondition & ResidualObligation model cross-body coordination failure and its statutory resolution.
SEMANTICThe SKOS scheme (right) gives a controlled vocabulary for consistent cross-system interpretation.
TECHNICALOWL 2 DL profile — compatible with Jena / Stardog / GraphDB & SPARQL; integrable with EUR-Lex and Ombudsman case systems.

SKOS Concept Scheme Meinhardt Governance Failure Vocabulary

Nullification LoopFive-stage administrative attractor producing non-determination regardless of personnel. related → Normative Collapse, Silence Veto
Normative CollapseStructural condition: valid procedural outputs, permanently unmet substantive obligation.
Silence VetoProcedural silence as substantive adverse determination — no notice, hearing, or reasons. Engages CFR Art.41.
Structural DisplacementCompetence distributed with no institutional subject able to determine. narrower → Ontic Collapse
Ontic CollapseAcute form: no institutional subject capable of determination exists at all. broader → Structural Displacement
