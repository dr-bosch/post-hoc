Section I · #phenomenon

The Phenomenon

A statutory obligation circulating indefinitely through a network of formally valid procedural outputs without any node holding the obligation to produce a substantive determination.

A sovereign Operations Research corpus with documented dual-use characteristics was submitted to Irish State institutions beginning May 2006[cite: 1, 2]. Across the full period, zero evaluations and zero statutory determinations have been produced[cite: 2].

The control framework is administered under EU Regulation 2021/821, with DETE acting as the National Competent Authority and licensing authority[cite: 4]. The architecture distributes functions across multiple departments without an integrated statutory coordination mechanism.

This condition constitutes a persistent procedural loop across successive governments and parliamentary terms with an invariant output: acknowledgement without determination[cite: 2].
