# Working Research Notebook: Initial Five-Document Corpus

**Status:** Provisional working notes  
**Scope:** Initial semiotic–POSIWID research preparation only  
**Method:** These notes identify questions and documentary features to examine. They do not establish that the documents' assertions are true, do not infer motives, and do not draw system-level conclusions.

## Working discipline

- **Observation:** What the document explicitly contains, including its form, date, wording, named actors, and stated sequence.
- **Interpretation:** A possible analytical reading of an observed feature.
- **Inference:** A proposition that would require comparison with other evidence; none is adopted here.
- **Hypothesis:** A provisional explanation requiring further evidence; none is treated as established.
- **Evidence gap:** Information not supplied by the document or not yet checked against an independent source.

The five documents are primarily submissions, briefs, annotations, or proposed frameworks rather than independent institutional records. They may therefore be especially useful for analysing representation and claims about behaviour, but their accounts require corroboration before being treated as evidence of events.

- **Observation:** It names or refers to DETE, DFA, the Department of Defence, the Office of Emergency Planning, the European Commission, the Ombudsman, Oireachtas committees, and named ministers.

### Analytical question

How does a formal notice construct responsibility and a duty to determine when it addresses a minister and describes functions distributed across several departments?

### Semiotic features to examine

- **Observation:** The letter repeatedly uses legal and procedural signifiers such as “competent authority,” “delegation,” “coordination,” “determination,” “obligation,” “transfer,” and “evaluation.”
- **Observation:** It frames the matter through oppositions including designation / discharge, authority / coordination, determination / non-determination, and transfer / continuity.
- **Observation:** It addresses the minister directly and ends with enumerated requests, creating an explicit addressee–requester relationship.
- **Interpretive question:** Examine whether agency is assigned directly to named office-holders, to departments as institutions, or to an architecture represented as lacking a responsible decision-maker.
- **Interpretive question:** Examine how the AI Office is used as a comparative institutional signifier and how EU law is used as an authority-bearing intertext.
- **Evidence gap:** The letter's legal characterisations and descriptions of departmental practice are assertions within this document; the underlying instruments, correspondence, and official responses have not been independently checked here.

### Behavioural features to examine

- **Observation:** The letter presents a chronology involving earlier submissions, departmental acknowledgements, a transfer of designation, a Defence-related determination, an Ombudsman complaint, and a pending or open Commission evaluation.
- **Analytical question:** For each claimed event, identify the date, actor, input, action, response, and documented consequence, then compare the account with primary records.
- **Analytical question:** Distinguish actions attributed to an institution from the letter's assessment of what those actions legally accomplished.
- **Evidence gap:** The letter does not itself establish whether every named event occurred as described or whether the cited legal effects follow.

### Possible non-decision, referral, delay, closure, or recurrence

- **Observation:** The letter expressly describes a long-running absence of a statutory determination and refers to acknowledgements, transfers, a purported Defence closure, and an unanswered rebuttal.
- **Observation:** It asks whether a determination has been issued or is in preparation and whether a mechanism exists to assemble cross-departmental inputs.
- **Interpretive question:** Test whether the cited sequence meets the protocol's conditions for a non-decision, rather than treating the phrase “non-determination” as conclusive.
- **Interpretive question:** Test whether the reported transfer and successive institutional responses form a referral or routing sequence.
- **Evidence gap:** A complete event record, response dates, applicable deadlines, and any final legally operative determination are not contained in the letter alone.

### Provisional status

No inference about the Department's purpose, motive, or system function is made. This document is suitable as a claim-bearing notice whose propositions should be decomposed and corroborated.
