## Selection caveat

This is a corpus-selection survey, not an analysis of the documents. The presence of a document, heading, category, or asserted chronology does not establish that the underlying event or pattern occurred. Any subsequent analysis should identify the source, preserve relevant wording, distinguish observation from interpretation, define the system boundary, record uncertainty, and preserve competing explanations where the evidence permits them.
