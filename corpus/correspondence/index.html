INTERPRETATION

The corpus consistently characterises each of these as "closure without determination" rather than as an adverse determination on the merits. Whether each instance above meets the non-decision test — identifiable input, identifiable recipient, identifiable duty, elapsed time, absence of concluding decision — is not adjudicated here. It requires the primary instrument (the actual Section 92 resolution text, the actual JCEUA committee record) rather than the corpus's paraphrase of it.

Evidence gap: primary committee/departmental records are not held in this repository; only the corpus's characterisation of them is.
