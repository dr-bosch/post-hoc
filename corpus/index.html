corpus/

The documentary base: primary source documents. Nothing in this directory is analytical; it is the material the rest of the repository decomposes. Source wording is preserved unaltered.

Subdirectories

  • codes and practice/ — formal codes, guidance documents, and procedural standards bearing on institutional conduct
  • correspondence/ — letters and notes addressed to named institutions/officials
  • decisions/ — institutional acts characterised in the corpus as determinations, declinations, or closures
  • institutional-records/ — reference tables: committee matrix, deadlines, contact logs
  • statements/ — self-authored statements of record, briefs, and published essays

Evidence gap: none of the documents below are independent institutional records (minutes, official gazettes, court filings). They are submissions about such records. See synthesis/system-analysis.md.
