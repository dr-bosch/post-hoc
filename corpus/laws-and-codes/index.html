laws-and-codes/

Primary normative texts. Legislation, regulations, codes, and related instruments that constitute the legal and regulatory frameworks within which institutional action is represented as authorised, required, constrained, or prohibited. Texts are preserved as source material; analysis belongs elsewhere in the repository.

Subdirectories

  • primary-legislation/ — Acts and other primary legislative texts establishing powers, duties, institutions, and legal authority
  • statutory-instruments/ — regulations, orders, and other instruments made under delegated legislative authority
  • eu-regulations/ — directly applicable EU regulations and associated instruments relevant to the corpus
  • eu-directives/ — EU directives establishing obligations or frameworks requiring implementation or transposition
  • codes-of-practice/ — formal codes, guidance, and procedural standards bearing on institutional conduct

Semiotic relevance

These texts are examined not only for what they prescribe, but for the categories through which institutional reality is constituted: competence, obligation, discretion, authority, procedure, responsibility, exception, and remedy. Their normative language provides a distinct layer against which institutional representations and observable behaviour may subsequently be compared.

Evidence gap: none of the documents below are independent institutional records (minutes, official gazettes, court filings). They are submissions about such records. See synthesis/system-analysis.md.
