Recurring outputs of the system, named without reference to intent. Each observation states a pattern and its frequency. Where the FSD ontology provides a SWRL rule, the rule is applied to the existing record.

fsd:AdministrativeAmnesia (FSD-Ontology-2026 §4.4)
Pattern: each transfer resets the institutional clock to zero while the underlying fsd:nonDeterminationPeriodMonths continues to accumulate. The receiving body has no formal record of the submission, producing fresh cycles of superficial engagement.
source: FSD-Ontology-2026.md §4.4

Recurring output: OEP three positions, none reconciled

OBSERVATION The OEP has three recorded positions on the corpus across 19 years: (1) May 2006 — Rooney: State interest acknowledged, submission invited, processing blocked on credentials condition, no referral. (2) Jul 2009 — Kelliher: declined on remit grounds. (3) Jun 2025 — McMoreland: substantive adequacy conclusion. None reconciles the prior positions. The McEntee Foreclosure relies on (3) without acknowledging (1) or (2).
source: Interregnum-nullificans-v13.md §IV
INTERPRETATION Three irreconcilable positions from the same institutional body across 19 years is the corpus's strongest case for fsd:NullificationLoop as a pattern of acts rather than a sequence of individually defensible decisions. The pattern is: each position is locally defensible; the aggregate is logically incoherent; the Foreclosure uses the most recent position as though the prior two do not exist. This is fsd:AdministrativeAmnesia applied not across bodies but within a single body.

Structural condition: three-institution fragmentation

fsd:StructuralDisplacement → fsd:OnticCollapse (FSD-Ontology-2026 §4.5)
SWRL Rule 6: established when (a) no single body holds the full competence required and (b) no statutory mechanism assembles the partial competences. Applied to the existing record:
— DETE: licensing, CA designation
— DFA: Wassenaar / NSG / MTCR / Australia Group regime memberships
— DoD / OEP: security assessment architecture
No statute requires these three to consult before a determination. An integrated dual-use assessment requires all three simultaneously. Per Rule 6: fsd:OnticCollapse is instantiated.
source: FSD-Ontology-2026.md §7, §12; Interregnum-nullificans-v13.md §V
INFERENCE Whether the integrated assessment actually requires all three inputs simultaneously — or whether DETE could discharge the obligation with the information it holds, consulting the others on an ad hoc basis — is the institutional design question the corpus treats as closed and this repository treats as open pending a court or Ombudsman determination.
