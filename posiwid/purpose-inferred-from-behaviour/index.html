posiwid/purpose-inferred-from-behaviour/

Candidate explanations for the recurring output pattern documented in system-observations/. Alternatives are listed rather than one selected. Intentionality is not inferred from outcome alone.

Explanation A: Structural / architectural

OBSERVATION The three-institution fragmentation (DETE / DFA / DoD) with no statutory coordination mechanism means an integrated CA assessment is architecturally unavailable — not because any actor chose to withhold it but because no institutional pathway for assembling the required inputs exists.
source: feasible-region.html §02; Interregnum-nullificans-v13.md §V; FSD-Ontology-2026.md §7 (SWRL Rule 6)
INTERPRETATION Hart reading (feasible-region.html §09): "No node holds the obligation to recognise that the collective function has not been performed" — restated for constitutional architecture from Hart's incomplete contracts theory. The system produces non-determination not by anyone's decision but because the architecture is silent on who holds the residual right when every node has correctly discharged only its component. Prescription: name a holder of the residual right. The Qualifying Submissions Bill does this via the Ombudsman's mandatory s.10C(4) application.
See also: semiotics/narratives/ (Hart) · semiotics/signifiers/ (Ontic Collapse)

Explanation B: Cognitive / organisational

OBSERVATION feasible-region.html §01 OR note (updated): Simon's bounded rationality — organisations satisfice, search stops at "adequate." The sufficiency assertion is that stopping point formalised. Cyert & March: search begins only when a problem is felt as a problem; organisational slack absorbs discomfort without the friction reaching a decision-maker with authority to act. A submission no single node is under pressure to resolve never generates enough friction to be searched for.
source: feasible-region.html §01 (updated OR note)
INTERPRETATION This explanation requires no bad faith and no coordination between actors. It predicts exactly the observed pattern (acknowledgement → satisficing closure at each node → no determination) from premises that are independently well-supported in organisational theory. It is the explanation most consistent with the fsd:NullificationLoop being "personnel-independent" — if the pattern is driven by cognitive and organisational dynamics rather than intent, it would recur regardless of who held each role.

Explanation C: Public goods / incentive failure

OBSERVATION feasible-region.html §09 (Pigouvian note): Sovereign analytical capacity is a public good. Its non-production is a market failure: the cost of evaluation falls on a single submitter; the benefit is non-excludable across the population; no individual node captures enough of the collective benefit to make evaluation individually rational.
source: feasible-region.html §09
INTERPRETATION Under this explanation, the system is doing exactly what its incentive structure produces: each node rationally withholds the cost of evaluation because it cannot capture the benefit. The Pigouvian prescription is to internalise the externality — a statutory cost attached to non-determination makes evaluation individually rational. No collective failure of will is required; only a missing price signal.

Explanation D: Triage / volume (alternative not ruled out)

HYPOTHESIS A very high-volume, multi-recipient correspondence campaign (220 Oireachtas members, 14 MEPs, multiple ministers, multiple committees — all simultaneously) may be processed by secretariat functions as lobby correspondence rather than as a statutory submission requiring individual determination. Under this explanation, the "silence" data is consistent with routine triage rather than deliberate non-engagement.
INFERENCE The corpus does not rule out Explanation D. Its response is to argue that the form of submission (invoking specific EU regulations by article number, attaching a 226-page complaint, engaging by reference to specific statutory obligations) distinguishes the corpus from routine lobby correspondence. Whether the distinction was legible to each receiving secretariat is not established by the corpus's account.
Evidence gap: no internal routing classification from any receiving body is in this repository. The difference between Explanations B, C, and D is not resolvable from the corpus alone.
OBSERVATION The corpus contains an internal self-correction ("Articles that do not hold as claimed" — logged against its own earlier legal arguments in meinhardt-initiative-compact-sep10.md). That the corpus is capable of revising its own claims under scrutiny is itself relevant evidence about the quality of analysis being applied to the record.
source: meinhardt-initiative-compact-sep10.md (Critical Legal Clarification)
