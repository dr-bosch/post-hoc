behaviour/non-actions/

Dated record of what did not happen — evaluations not produced, reports not delivered, determinations not issued. Chronological. Each entry states only what the record directly shows; absence is recorded as absence, not as proved intent.

fsd:NonDetermination (OWL class · FSD-Ontology-2026 §4.3)

The ontology defines this as: a public body having received a qualifying submission but not issued a determination within 12 months. Established by conduct — silence, acknowledgement, sufficiency assertion, and administrative amnesia each instantiate this class. Disjoint with fsd:Determination.
