behaviour/referrals/

Record of instances where a body characterised the submission as belonging to another body's remit. The FSD ontology distinguishes lawful referral (which satisfies the duty) from remit displacement (which does not, because no destination is identified and no transmission is made).

fsd:RemitDisplacement vs fsd:ReferralDetermination (FSD-Ontology-2026 §4.4 / §4.3)

Lawful referral: identifies the competent body by name, transmits the submission within 30 days, notifies the submitter. Satisfies the duty of determination.
Remit displacement: characterises the matter as belonging elsewhere without: (a) identifying the body; (b) transmitting; or (c) notifying. Does not satisfy the duty.

source: FSD-Ontology-2026.md §4.3, §4.4
