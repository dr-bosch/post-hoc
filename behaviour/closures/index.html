behaviour/closures/

Dated record of institutional acts that closed a submission, correspondence, or proceeding. Each entry records the act, its stated basis (where known), and what question it left unresolved. Register: legal chronology.

fsd:ForeClosureByProxy (OWL class · FSD-Ontology-2026 §4.4)

Defined as: a purported determination issued by a body that (a) held no CA designation at the time of submission; (b) relies exclusively on conclusions of a body also without CA designation; (c) does not engage formally rebutted conclusions. Named instance: DOD-MO-01201-2025.

fsd:DesignedForgetting (OWL class · FSD-Ontology-2026 §4.4)

Active deletion of formally lodged correspondence without: (a) issuing a determination; (b) transmitting to a competent body; or (c) placing on the formal record. Named instance: JCEUA deletion, October 2025. Engages Art. 41 CFR directly.
