Interregnum Nullificans

20 years without determination · Ireland · 2006–2026

A twenty-year evidentiary record of administrative non-decision within the Irish State. Submissions received, retained, unacted upon. The duty to reason has not been discharged. This corpus is public, CC0, and structured as a saisine: the authority, once notified, cannot decline the jurisdictional obligation that attaches on receipt.

These are not labels. They are directional mechanisms — each grounded in a named act or omission on the record. Together they encode every link in the provenance chain of the Interregnum Nullificans corpus from May 2006 to the Presidency Paradox opening 1 July 2026.

The relations divide into two registers. The OWL object properties — :ignores through :amplifies — encode the primary causal chain of the Nullification Loop: what the institution did to the submission, what the loop produced, and what the campaign made of the record. The provenance relations — :derivedFrom through :noDetermination — encode the evidentiary structure that makes the causal chain legally operable: who is responsible, when they acted, what the act generated, and what its absence means. A causal chain establishes that something happened. A provenance chain establishes who must answer for it. Both are required for the remedy to run.

:ignores
AdministrativeProcess → Submission
Every submission carries a demand — not merely to be received but to be answered. The demand is the condition of the submission's meaning within any governance relationship that claims legitimacy: the act of submitting is also the act of extending a claim to a body with the competence to adjudicate it. Where the submission is acknowledged but not processed, a relation has been performed without its substance. The administrative form of receipt has been enacted while the epistemic obligation — to read what was submitted on its own terms, to bring relevant knowledge to bear on it, and to produce a response on grounds that hold — has been withheld. Across nineteen years, each point in the notice chain was received in this mode: acknowledged, filed, and not read in the sense that reading requires. The loop's initiating operator is not the absence of action. It is the systematic substitution of the administrative form of engagement for its substance.

:suppresses
Actor → CivicSignal
Where :ignores describes non-processing, :suppresses names active institutional management of the record. Suppression is not passive — it requires a choice, and the choice leaves a trace. The JCEUA's deletion of formally received correspondence in October 2025 is the acute instance: a body charged with EU affairs oversight received a submission engaging ten EU-law dimensions and then, rather than forwarding or declining on stated grounds, erased the administrative trace. What remains — in the GitHub repositories, the drive archives, this page, before the European Ombudsman — is the record the institution tried to reduce to nothing. The trace outlasts the management. Every act of suppression generates its own evidence: the deletion is itself a datum. The record has grown in proportion to the effort made to contain it. That proportion is not accidental; it is the structural consequence of suppressing a publicly indexed corpus.

:producesFailure
Process → FailureMode
The loop's diagnostic property is its personnel-independence. No individual bad faith is required; no single actor need have designed the outcome. Each node processes its own function correctly — the reference number is issued, the acknowledgement formatted, the deferral correctly routed — and the collective obligation fails. This is not malfunction. It is stable self-regulation producing a pathological output: a system that maintains equilibrium precisely by converting every epistemic input into administrative closure, while the corrective signal that would indicate the evaluation function has not been performed finds no path through the architecture. Where a viable governance system transmits signals of unmet function upward until they trigger corrective action, this architecture produces local validity at every node while the collective obligation evaporates. The same output — acknowledgement-without-determination — has been produced across six Taoisigh, four governments, and multiple institutional restructurings. The equilibrium is the pathology.

:triggers
FailureMode → LegalCondition
A failure mode activates a legally cognisable condition not because it intends to, but because the law has named the output and prescribed a response. The McEntee Foreclosure of February 4th, 2026 is the instance: it converted the loop's cumulative output into a dated, attributable, reviewable act — the handle by which Order 84 RSC is engaged. The Foreclosure did not create the legal condition; it crystallised what the record already contained. Every acknowledgement-without-determination across nineteen years was already legally operative; the Foreclosure made the structure actionable by attaching it to a named decision by a named minister on a named date, within the judicial review window, on three independent irrationality grounds. The law does not require that an actor have known what they were triggering. It requires only that the triggering act be established and the grounds for review be shown. The act did what it did; the record shows what it was.

:violates
Process → Right
The duty to give reasons is not a procedural formality. It is the constitutional form of the governance condition that distinguishes a legitimate polity from mere administration: the obligation to address the person before you on grounds that are relevant, rather than on grounds of administrative convenience, and to do so in time to make a difference. Four Article 41 CFR guarantees are violated independently on the face of the record: impartial timely handling — nineteen years eliminates any claim to reasonable time; the right to be heard before any adverse measure — the Section 8(3) determination without hearing; the right of access to one's file — the JCEUA deletion; the obligation to give reasons — zero produced across the full period. Each guarantee is independent of the others. Together they constitute not an accumulation but a single condition: the State acted on the petitioner's submissions without having engaged them. The form of governance was performed. Its substance was not.

:amplifies
Campaign → CivicSignal
Amplification is the act that places the record beyond institutional reach. Where :suppresses names the institution's management of the record, :amplifies names the campaign's response: the conversion of what the institution sought to reduce to a private administrative exchange into a public datum — visible, arguable, criticisable. Each GitHub repository is a timestamped, CC0-licensed, permanently indexed entry in the public record. The institution can close a file. It cannot close a file that has been publicly indexed in fourteen EU languages and transmitted simultaneously to PETI, the European Ombudsman, and the Commission's mandatory evaluation. The asymmetry is structural: the campaign's cost of amplification is near zero; the institution's cost of suppressing an amplified record is near infinite. This is not an accident of the digital medium. It is the condition under which institutional silence becomes publicly legible as silence — and not merely as an absence that no one has noticed.

:derivedFrom
Instrument · Submission · Collection → LegalInstrument · Record · Document
An instrument is derived from prior documents in the sense that it inherits their grounds — it draws its legal basis, its factual content, its analytical structure from what came before, and carries that inheritance as a verifiable chain. The Tairiscint Mheinhardt v10 is derived from the O.R. corpus, the Nullification Record, the McEntee Foreclosure, the Gunn Letter, the Memorandum to Government, and the case authorities O'Keeffe and Meadows — simultaneously, not sequentially. Each derivation is a point at which the instrument can be tested against its source: does the claim follow from the record? Is the ground established by the document it cites? The derivation chain is also the hermeneutic chain: the instrument takes what it has received, brings it into a new context — the context of legal remedy — and transforms it by that act of bringing. A derived instrument is not merely assembled from parts. It is an interpretation: it takes what came before and makes something new answerable to the same grounds.

:attributedTo
Document · Submission · Instrument → Person · Minister
Attribution is what converts an institutional act into a reviewable act by a named person. Without attribution, a document belongs to a system, and a system has no accountability; with attribution, it belongs to a decision-maker who must answer for what was decided. The McEntee Foreclosure is attributed to Minister Helen McEntee — it is she whose decision is unreasoned on three independent grounds, she who is the certiorari target. The Martin Ballyphehane commitment of 2009 is attributed to Minister Micheál Martin — it is he whose unperformed obligation is the seventeen-and-a-half-year absence at the centre of the record. Attribution names the responsible actor within the institutional story in a way that makes individual accountability legible without reducing the systemic failure to individual misconduct. Both are present. The attribution chain holds both without collapsing them into each other.

:onBehalfOf
Person · Minister · Official → Department · Organisation
Acting on behalf of is the relation that makes institutional authority and individual responsibility simultaneously present — and simultaneously implicated. The minister acts on behalf of the State; the State acts on behalf of the public; the public is the beneficiary-public denied the analytical resource the corpus would have provided. The chain of representation that makes governance legitimate is also the chain through which its failure propagates: each link that substitutes procedural motion for substantive engagement reproduces the failure at the next level. What flows back through this relation is not only authority but the obligation that authority carries with it. Minister McEntee acts on behalf of DOD and DFA; her Foreclosure is therefore the departments' act; their act is the State's act; the State's act is the government's act. Seventeen and a half years of unperformed obligation are distributed across this relation, office by office, minister by minister, each acting correctly in their own procedural code, the collective obligation evaporating at every node.

:hasMember

The corpus is a bundle — not an archive, not a collection, but a legally coherent whole whose members establish the grounds of the claims by their collective presence. :generatedBy

A document was generated by a named activity on a named date. The act crystallises into a text, and the text carries more meaning than the act intended. That surplus of meaning is precisely what the remedy tracks deploy.

Citizen's Bulletin · Early-warning signs · Government by impasse
Paper trail without follow-through
Acknowledgements, receipts, file numbers — and nothing moves. Ask: What changed after this receipt? If the filing cabinet got heavier but nothing else happened, that is a design fault, not a clerical delay.
→ Hardcopy antinomy
Roles that encode contradiction
Offices tasked to do A and not-A simultaneously, with no protected channel for dissent. When exit, voice, and loyalty are each blocked by job design, the result is symbolic performance, not correction.
→ Structural red flag
Learning available on paper, impossible in practice
The methods exist but no institutional pathway to use them. Problems repeat and get explained away. The complaint calls this deutero-binding — structures that prevent learning about learning (after Gregory Bateson).
→ Annex IV · Dáil mechanics
Frozen field decision-making
Everyone agrees the issue is complex yet all routes are ruled out by mandate, etiquette, or timing. Meetings multiply; action doesn't. Persistent freeze is a systems problem, not a scheduling issue.
→ Incentives punish correction
Evidence archived instead of acted on
High-resolution submissions loop between offices; portfolio holders rotate; the record grows — but nothing converts information into agency. When the archive beats the action every time, the archive is doing political work.
→ Archive as accountability sink
Cross-border obligations noted, never activated
Emails flagging humanitarian coordination, ECHR obligations, or emergency readiness — received, cc'd — yet no joint mechanism triggered. Once risk is flagged across borders, non-activation widens humanitarian and rights exposure.
